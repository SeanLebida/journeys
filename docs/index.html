Six Ways to Explore

⟿

Route Tracing

Follow animated historical routes on the ancient map. Trace the patriarchs, the exodus, the conquest, the kings, and the ministry journeys of Jesus across Galilee and Judea, followed by Paul’s missionary voyages to Rome.

◍

Sandbox Atlas

Freely explore the ancient Mediterranean and Near East with toggleable historical layers. Inspect Roman roads, trade lanes, province borders, and ancient settlements with rich topographical context.

≣

Biblical Timeline

One unified chronology from Creation through the apostolic age. Each era uses its own proportional scale so Passion week is not flattened against three millennia. Parallel swimlanes track figures, prophets, kings, battles, and finds.

▤

Embedded Bible Reader

Read Scripture alongside the map. Complete public-domain texts (King James Version & American Standard Version) bundled in a fast on-device SQLite database. Tap place names in Scripture to jump straight to their location on the map.

❖

Guided Lessons

Paced deep dives into biblical events, examining cultural context, topography, and archaeological artifacts step-by-step.

?

Interactive Quizzes

Test and sharpen your knowledge of ancient geography, apostolic travels, and biblical chronology with interactive map-pin challenges.

Archaeology & Academic Rigor

Cited Claims

Every site and route stop is anchored to primary historical texts (Scripture, Josephus, Tacitus, cuneiform annals, Roman itineraries) and excavation records. Over 1,164 citations with transparent confidence ratings.

Rival Candidates Shown

When scholars disagree on a biblical site’s exact location, Journeys displays documented rivals as hollow markers rather than arbitrarily guessing a single pin.

Graded Route Strokes

Surveyed Roman roads, reconstructed travel corridors, sea lanes, and schematic connectors are drawn distinctly so reconstructions are never mistaken for surveyed roads.

Designed for Privacy & Offline Study

Journeys works anywhere — in church, in the classroom, on flights, or in the field. The entire app shell, map tiles, and complete Bible database are stored directly on your device. We do not track you, collect personal data, or sell anything.
