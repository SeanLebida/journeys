# iOS app icon is the Journeys compass

## What changed

- `scripts/generate-assets.ts --ios-icon` renders `public/icon.svg` into `ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-512@2x.png` and `public/splash.svg` into the three `Splash.imageset` PNGs.
- The marketing icon is 1024×1024, PNG color type 2, no `tRNS`, not interlaced. The SVG squircle (`rx="108"`) and the gold rim are dropped so the night background fills the square. iOS applies the mask.
- The launch image is the portrait splash centered on a 2732×2732 `#070a10` square. `LaunchScreen.storyboard` still uses `scaleAspectFill`.
- The product-page header composite now masks that icon to an app-icon corner radius. `node scripts/capture-app-store-assets.mjs marketing` rewrites `docs/store-assets/apple/header-3840x1646.png` from the existing screenshots.

## Why it mattered

TestFlight build 140 and the App Store asset catalog were still the Capacitor blue cross on a white grid. Android mipmaps already used the compass. App Store Connect shows the icon from the uploaded binary, so the catalog change does not appear until a newer build is processed.

## Gotchas / pitfalls

- Do not leave the SVG's rounded rect in the 1024 file. Transparent corners fail App Store validation, and a second rounding makes a double mask.
- The generator throws if the `rx="108"` background rect is gone, instead of shipping a rounded icon by accident.
- `ios:version:bump` sets `CFBundleVersion` from `git rev-list --count HEAD`. Build 140 is the commit count of `1f85b3d`. The next archive from this branch must stay above 140. Do not commit the bumped `project.pbxproj` from CI.
- The header in App Store Connect was uploaded earlier and still shows the Capacitor mark until that image is replaced. The search image does not include the icon.

## Verification

- Corner pixel of the 1024 icon is `#090d16` (9, 13, 22). Center pixel is the white compass jewel, not the Capacitor blue.
- Splash corner is `#070a10`. The preview shows the compass, the route, and the Journeys wordmark.
- Header crop shows the rounded compass above the word Journeys, on `#0a0a0a`, with no alpha.
- No local `xcodebuild`. The home-screen icon changes only after the next TestFlight build from this commit.
