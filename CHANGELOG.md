# Changelog

All notable changes to Journeys are documented in this file.

## [0.3.15] - 2026-10-01

• Close place details when you move the map or tap elsewhere.
• Map place-detail sits below sheets and chrome (no overlay covering UI).

## [0.3.14] - 2026-10-01

• Tap a rival identification to find that location on the map.
• Place sheets keep the site centered in the visible map area (sheet / rail / sidebar offset), not under the description.
• Quiz “place a pin” peeks the sheet so the map is tappable.
• External map links open on Android via the system browser.
• PWA install metas and a web/Android/iOS platform checklist.

## [0.3.13] - 2026-09-22

• Timeline lists the birth of Jesus (Bethlehem, Egypt, the return, the temple at age twelve) and the ministry through the Ascension, with patriarchs and Old Testament battles.
• Battles that fall close together no longer cover one another, so each one can be opened.
• Store listing for the next closed test describes that whole record.

## [0.3.12] - 2026-09-07

Initial testing release of Journeys — Atlas of the Apostles:
• Interactive Route Tracing: Animated journeys of Jesus & Paul.
• Sandbox Atlas: Roman roads, province borders & archaeology.
• Biblical Timeline: Chronology from Creation through Rome.
• Offline Bible Reader: KJV & ASV text linked to the map.
• Guided Lessons & Quizzes: Biblical geography and history.
• 100% Offline: No accounts, no ads, zero tracking.
